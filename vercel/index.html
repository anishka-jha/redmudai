Model, equations & assumptions

This is a bounded exponential surrogate, not a Darcy-law or cake-resistance model. Solids concentration is mass based. All solids remain in the cake, water density is 1,000 kg/m³, and there is no evaporation, leakage or dissolution.

dry_solids = batch_kg × solids_pct / 100
initial_water = batch_kg − dry_solids
removable_water = max(0, initial_water − 0.35 × dry_solids)
tau_min = 18 × (solids_pct / 30)^1.5 × (batch_kg / 1000)^0.5
recovered_water = removable_water × [1 − exp(−time_min × sqrt(pressure_bar) / tau_min)]
cake_water = initial_water − recovered_water
recovery_pct = 100 × recovered_water / initial_water
moisture_pct = 100 × cake_water / (dry_solids + cake_water)
energy_kWh = pressure_bar × 100000 × (recovered_water / 1000) / (0.60 × 3600000)
             + 0.25 × time_min / 60

The 0.35 kg/kg water floor, 18-minute coefficient and concentration/loading exponents are invented. Fixed filter area is assumed. Energy assumes 60% hydraulic efficiency and 0.25 kW auxiliary power. It excludes feed/cake pressurization, compressed air and upstream/downstream energy.

Pressure-dependent cake compression, permeability, particle size, temperature, chemistry and water reuse quality are excluded. No calibration data or uncertainty intervals are available. “Optimal” means best within the chosen grid and score only.
